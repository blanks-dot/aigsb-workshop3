Six habits that save you an hour

  1. New chat when it gets messy. Long chats get foggy, like a crowded counter. Clear it and start fresh. Your skills come with you.
  2. Talk, don't type. Hit the mic and ramble. You'll say more and it sounds more like you. Mary's fave: Wispr Flow, free plan.
  3. Tell it who's reading. A recruiter? A 10-year-old? Your mom? Say it before it writes.
  4. Show, don't describe. Paste a screenshot or a site you love instead of hunting for adjectives.
  5. Fix the skill, not the page. Same fix twice? Say add this to my skill so it sticks for good.
  6. Push it to think, before acting. "Before you build, ask me 3 questions and tell me your plan."
