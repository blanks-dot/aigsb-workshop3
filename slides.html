Left alone, AI drifts to the average

Hintze et al., 2025

Why? Each round drops what's unusual and fills the gap with what's most common. 100 rounds = visual elevator music

- Researchers played telephone with AI. One model describes an image, another redraws it from the description, 100 times over. - 1,000 different starting images ended up as 12 kinds of picture: lighthouses, cities at night, cozy kitchens. - Each description drops what's unusual, and each redraw fills the gap with the most common version. - They called the result visual elevator music. Your bio and your site drift the same way unless you steer. (Hintze et al., Patterns, 2025)

SCHILKE & REIMANN 2025
